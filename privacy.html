Information we collect on this Site

This Site does not include forms, user accounts, advertising trackers, or analytics cookies. We do not collect personal information through the Site itself. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request logs to deliver and secure the Site.

Third-party applications

Buttons and QR codes on this Site link to applications operated by third parties:

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those services before submitting information.

If you email us or submit a partner application, we use that information to respond to you, evaluate referral partnerships, coordinate referrals with Sunrun, and administer referral commissions. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to request correction or deletion, subject to applicable law.
