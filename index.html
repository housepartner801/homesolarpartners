    Solar paired with batteries brings peace of mind.

For roofing companies

Help your customers get up to $10,000 toward a new roof1

Qualified homeowners may receive a Sunrun subsidy toward their roof replacement after their Sunrun home energy system is installed. You handle the roof project; Sunrun explains the energy options and any rebates the homeowner qualifies for.

  • The homeowner pays you for the roof, as usual
  • Any approved Sunrun subsidy is paid after the solar and battery installation
