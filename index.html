FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Homeowners

How does the solar process work?

Start by scanning the homeowner QR code or using the application link. Sunrun then contacts you, reviews your home and energy needs, and presents the options available to you. You review the proposal and decide whether to move forward. If you do, Sunrun handles design, permitting, and installation under your agreement with Sunrun.

Does it cost anything to explore my options?

No. Applying is free, and submitting the application doesn't commit you to anything. Any pricing, payments, or plan terms are presented by Sunrun in your proposal before you decide.

Will solar eliminate my electric bill?

Not necessarily. Most solar homes stay connected to the utility grid, and results depend on your system size, energy use, utility rates and policies, and the plan you choose. Sunrun can walk you through estimates for your home, but savings aren't guaranteed.

Can I add battery storage?

Sunrun offers home battery storage in many areas. Availability depends on your location, home, and program, so ask about battery options during your Sunrun consultation.

Who handles installation?

Sunrun. Sunrun handles the consultation, eligibility review, pricing, contract, installation, and ongoing customer relationship. Home Solar Partners is an independent referral partner and does not install solar systems.

Business Partners

How does the referral program work?

Submit the partner application. If approved, you'll receive the referral process to share with interested homeowners. Sunrun then works with those homeowners directly, and you may earn a commission when a referred customer completes a qualifying installation under the program terms.

What types of businesses can participate?

Businesses that already work with homeowners are a natural fit—real estate professionals, home improvement and roofing companies, HVAC companies, property service providers, and others. All participation is subject to approval.

Do I need solar experience?

No. Your role is to make the introduction. Sunrun handles the solar conversation, proposal, and installation.

Who handles the homeowner consultation?

Sunrun. Once a homeowner is referred, Sunrun manages the consultation, eligibility review, proposal, and installation process.

When are referral commissions earned?

Commissions are tied to qualifying completed installations, as defined in the program terms you receive at enrollment. Amounts, timing, and eligibility are set by the program and may change.
